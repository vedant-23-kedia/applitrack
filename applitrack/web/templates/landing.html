Your job applications, tracked without opening your inbox.

Connect your Gmail. AppliTrack finds every job you have applied to, works out whether each one is live, rejected or waiting on you, and keeps a spreadsheet in your own Google Drive up to date{% if realtime %} the moment a company replies{% else %} automatically{% endif %}.

Connect your Gmail  Free. Read-only. Disconnect anytime.

What you get

A spreadsheet you own

Created in your own Google Drive, with company, role, status, how long each one has been quiet, and a link straight to the email thread. Your own notes column is never overwritten.

An email when something changes

Rejected, moved to interview, sent an assessment — you hear about it without reading a single recruiter email.

How it works

  1. You connect Gmail. Google asks your permission; we never see your password.
  2. We read the last year of mail. Read-only — nothing can be sent, changed or deleted.
  3. The noise gets thrown away. Job boards send marketing that copies the wording of real emails — "Confirm your application", "Application update". Those are filtered out; real updates are kept.
  4. Your sheet appears, and the link arrives in your inbox.
  5. It stays current by itself{% if realtime %} — within seconds of a company emailing you{% endif %}.
